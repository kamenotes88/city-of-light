# Community data boundary

The authoritative notice is [privacy.html](privacy.html); participation terms
are [terms.html](terms.html), version `community-v1`. Free offline use requires
neither an account nor this consent. The preview stores only opt-in community
profiles, direct attribution, team memberships and deliberately entered map
summaries. No personal graph, chat contents or backup is automatically uploaded.

Auth belongs to the existing hosting project. Never remove or reconfigure its
users, email template, OAuth providers, redirects or other application policies
as a side effect of installing/removing City of Light. Requests to remove a
community profile require a reviewed, scoped process; the preview is not an
automated erasure service. Do not export identity-provider emails to GitHub.

Browser sessions are memory only. Owner setup credentials are private,
single-use and time limited; only their digests are stored on the server.
Infrastructure providers retain their own operational logs and policies.

No automated analytics, financial eligibility, payment flow, global online
directory or claim of a verified unique person follows from account counts.
