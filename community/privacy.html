Community privacy notice

Version community-v1 · October 11, 2026 · Operator: Serhii Chumachenko

What leaves your device

Opening the online portal contacts GitHub Pages. Requesting sign-in sends your email to the configured Supabase Auth service. Verification sends your one-time credential to that service. The City of Light account database keeps an Auth account ID, display name, referral code, acknowledged terms version and timestamps. The operator's community list does not expose email addresses; the identity provider necessarily holds them.

The service stores your accepted direct inviter, team memberships, outstanding invitation hashes and expiry, explicitly entered city titles, locations, kinds and declared states. It records dated administrative, attribution and membership events for review. It never automatically uploads your local projects, graph, AI chats, browser backups or credentials.

Who can see it

You see your own direct inviter and direct invitees' community display names and account IDs. Team members see their shared city and members. The authorized operator can inspect registered profiles and direct referral relationships for support and later reconciliation. Infrastructure administrators and providers process data as part of hosting and identity service operation. Teams are private to their members; there is no public list of accounts or cities.

Browser storage and requests

Login access and refresh tokens stay only in this page's memory and are cleared on logout. Reloading requires sign-in again. A pending referral code may stay in this tab's session storage until accepted or cleared; it is not proof of identity. Team-access and private owner-activation tokens stay only in memory. Invitation fragments are removed from the address before service requests.

There are no advertising cookies or fingerprinting analytics in this portal. A visible team map checks for updates every 20 seconds and stops while hidden, logged out or offline. GitHub and Supabase may keep their own operational access and security logs; this application cannot erase provider logs or promise that providers collect nothing.

Retention and removal

Community records persist across sessions and reinstalls. A removal request requires ownership verification and operator review. It must cover memberships, shared contributions, attribution and audit records without affecting accounts in other applications sharing the identity service. Do not delete a shared Supabase Auth identity to remove only this community profile.

Use the account's removal-request control when available. For other support, open a GitHub issue without personal or sensitive details and ask how to verify ownership privately. No automatic erasure or response-time guarantee is offered in this preview. Keep a local copy of anything important; downloading a dashboard is not a full export of every provider record.

The product's local license does not grant permission to publish someone else's private information. Future analytics, public directories, payments or additional data sharing need their own disclosed policy and choices.
