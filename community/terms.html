Community preview terms

Version community-v1 · October 11, 2026

City of Light is provided by Serhii Chumachenko. These optional community features are a preview. The free local world remains available without an account, and its LICENSE continues to apply.

Your invitation

After verified sign-in and your acknowledgement, the service creates a stable community account and a personal invitation link. A new account may explicitly accept one direct inviter. The first accepted relationship is stored by the server; later links and reinstalls cannot replace it. This is account attribution, not proof of a unique human or seller eligibility.

Registration, invitations, downloads and playing do not earn money. No marketplace payments, licenses, commission accrual or payouts are active. Any later financial program requires separate published terms and acceptance. No future earnings are promised.

Team cities

A referral link grants no access to projects. A separate, expiring, single-use team invitation grants a viewer or editor role only after explicit acceptance. City owners can remove members and revoke outstanding invitations. Editors can create and edit their own entries; city owners can moderate them.

Shared entries are titles, locations and states you deliberately enter. They are visible to current team members and the authorized service operator. Do not add credentials, private client details or other people's sensitive information. A completed object is a team-declared result; it does not independently prove completion.

Synchronization operates while the community page is open. It does not run your AI chats, publish work, spend money or synchronize your whole local world. Existing private projects and backups stay on your device.

Preview limits and support

Accounts require a verified email identity. Do not share sign-in links, codes or team access links publicly. Use these features only for lawful work you are entitled to share. Availability depends on the hosting and identity providers and their usage limits; service continuity and unlimited capacity are not guaranteed.

For support, use GitHub Issues. Issues are public: never post passwords, sign-in links, email addresses, payment data or private project content. Security issues should use the repository's private vulnerability report.

Read the community privacy notice before joining. This preview has no online trading, chat, virtual payments or public city directory.
